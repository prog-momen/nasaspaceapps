# Gemini 4 Hydrogen

Gemini 4 Hydrogen (`gemini-4-hydrogen`) brings cutting-edge intelligence to everyone, with speed and efficiency for everyday coding and agentic workflows, featuring a unified 1M-token context window and long decode up to 1M tokens.
