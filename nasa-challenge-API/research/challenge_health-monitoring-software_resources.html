Space Agency Partner Resources

CSA (Canadian Space Agency)

Monitoring Radiation for Astronaut Health: Learn how the CSA is helping monitor space radiation to better protect astronaut health during long-duration missions. This resource highlights Canadian technologies and research used to measure radiation exposure in space and improve our understanding of its effects on the human body.

Why Do We Conduct Science Experiments in Space?: This CSA resource explains how the unique environment of space allows scientists to conduct experiments that cannot be performed on Earth. Learn how research aboard the International Space Station advances our understanding of human health, technology, and the universe. The discoveries made in space help improve life on Earth while supporting future exploration missions.
