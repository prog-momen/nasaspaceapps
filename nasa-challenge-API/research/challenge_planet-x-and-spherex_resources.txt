Space Agency Partner Resources
CSA (Canadian Space Agency)
NEOSSat: Observing Asteroids, Space Debris and Exoplanets
:
 Learn about NEOSSat, the world's first space telescope dedicated to detecting and tracking asteroids, comets, satellites, and space debris. This CSA resource highlights how Canada is helping protect our understanding of near-Earth space by contributing to international efforts to identify and monitor objects that could pose risks to Earth or spacecraft.
Canadian Astronomy Data Centre (CADC)
:
 The CADC provides access to a vast collection of astronomical observations from Canadian and international telescopes and space missions. Through this resource, participants can explore real scientific data used by astronomers to study stars, galaxies, exoplanets, asteroids, and other celestial objects.
NEOSSat Data Tutorial
:
 This tutorial demonstrates how to use Python to visualize data from Canada's NEOSSat mission. Follow step-by-step instructions to access spacecraft observations, create visualizations, and explore data collected by Canada's space telescope.
NEOSSat Dataset
:
 This dataset contains observations collected by NEOSSat, Canada's Near-Earth Object Surveillance Satellite. The data supports the detection and tracking of asteroids, comets, satellites, and space debris. Explore real observations from Canada's space telescope and learn how objects are monitored in near-Earth space.
