Digital games and activities
Digital games, e-books, and activities for all ages to learn more about space.
Tomatosphere Adventure
Play this Minecraft Education game to understand how photosynthesis works, and then grow tomatoes on the planet Mars!
Game – Mission: Astronaut
Complete missions and minigames on astronaut training, living and working in space, the Moon, the Webb Telescope, health and nutrition and much more!
Exploring Earth
Explore our planet using photos taken by astronauts from space and images from satellites.
Game – RADARSAT Constellation Mission
Help a farmer, a captain and a first responder  achieve their missions using RADARSAT imagery.
Game –
OSIRIS-REx
Help  the
OSIRIS-REx
spacecraft select the best site on asteroid Bennu to collect a  sample that will be brought to Earth.
The Explorers Club e-books
Launch on a space adventure through these stories inspired by our Canadian astronauts.
Quizzes
Test  your knowledge about space, science and astronauts with our fun quizzes.
3D printing models
Download files to 3D  print your own miniature models at your school, local library or maker space!
Astronaut cutouts
Curious to see what you would look like as an astronaut? Use our cutout feature to find out!
Puzzle: Canada from space
Assemble a digital puzzle of a mosaic of Canada  created with
RADARSAT-2
imagery.
Playlists
Discover new music or enjoy some nostalgic songs with the Canadian Space Agency's playlists.
