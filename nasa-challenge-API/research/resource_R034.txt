We believe that helping Canadian youth get excited about space and
STEM
is a shared responsibility. That's why our initiatives are tailored to the needs of teachers in the classroom, but also of
educators in more informal learning settings
and parents, whether or not they homeschool their kids.
So if you are looking for free, bilingual tools, activities and resources to build programming about space for the youth you work with, you've come to the right place!
